Privacy Notice

What we collect

Ground processes your text input to generate a structural reflection. Your input is sent to the AI model for processing and is not retained by the model provider for training purposes.

Local storage

Your reflection history is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to any server beyond what is needed for the initial processing of each reflection.

No training data

Your submissions are never used to train AI models. This is non-negotiable. Users describe real moral situations and disclose sensitive information; that information is processed and not retained.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share, or analyse your data. We do not build user profiles. We do not track you across sessions beyond a minimal usage counter (to enforce free-tier limits).

Contact

For questions about privacy, contact the project maintainer.

Ground — Moral Reflection Engine. Reflective, not prescriptive.
