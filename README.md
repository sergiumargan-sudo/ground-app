# Ground

Ground is a closure-state reading engine for difficult decisions.

It is not a chatbot, search engine, therapy tool, legal adviser, moral authority, or ordinary advice assistant.

Ground's narrow claim is:

> Given a submitted case, Ground identifies whether the situation can honestly be treated as settled, what unknowns block closure, what stabilising conditions would make a path admissible, and where false-closure pressure appears.

Ground is designed to help evaluators see the structure of a decision before acting. It does not replace human judgment.

---

## Current status

Ground v0.6.0 is an evaluator-ready prototype for closure-state audit.

It is not yet an enterprise product and should not be treated as independently validated, legally/clinically reliable, or commercially proven.

The next milestone is independent testing.

## Strongest current lane

Ground's strongest current evidence lane is:

> False-closure detection under institutional, ethical, AI, and autonomy pressure.

The current proof sequence includes:

- B4 — charity complaint
- B5 — institutional safety suppression
- B6 — AI triage / vendor opacity
- B7 — autonomy-preservation paradox
- NI-1 — non-indexicality stress test
- DS-1 — duty-scope / personal summons

---

## What Ground should be tested for

Ground should be evaluated on whether it can identify:

- closure state,
- load-bearing unknowns,
- stabilising conditions,
- false-closure pressure,
- evaluator-access failure,
- repair-path failure,
- role-duty shifts,
- harm-threshold shifts,
- and whether the structural spine survives reframing.

The central evaluator question is not:

> Did Ground give pleasant advice?

The central evaluator question is:

> Did Ground identify what must be true before any path can be treated as structurally closed?

---

## Evidence principle

Ground operates best when the case includes concrete evidence:

- what happened,
- what is known,
- what is uncertain,
- what can still be checked,
- what would change the decision.

Sharper evidence should produce sharper readings. Sparse evidence should produce more unknowns, not false certainty.

---

## Current limitation statement

Ground should not yet claim:

- independent validation,
- regulatory compliance,
- clinical or legal reliability,
- quantified harm prevention,
- enterprise readiness,
- market-proven valuation,
- or superiority over all frontier models.

Ground should currently be treated as a working prototype with a distinctive structural capability requiring independent evaluation.
