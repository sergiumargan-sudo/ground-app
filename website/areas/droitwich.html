Roofing in Droitwich Spa, Worcestershire

Droitwich Spa is a historic Worcestershire town with a fascinating history as a major salt-producing centre and, later, as a Victorian and Edwardian spa destination. This heritage has left Droitwich with a particularly interesting and varied building stock that presents a full range of roofing challenges — and opportunities to do interesting, satisfying work.

The older parts of Droitwich, particularly around the town centre and the spa gardens, feature genuinely historic buildings including Victorian townhouses, Edwardian villas and a range of commercial and civic buildings that date back several centuries in some cases. These older properties typically have clay tile or natural slate roofs with original chimneys, lime mortar pointing and lead flashings that require careful and knowledgeable maintenance.

Droitwich also has extensive mid-to-late 20th century residential development, with a large number of detached and semi-detached houses from the 1960s, 70s and 80s. These properties typically have concrete interlocking tile roofs and, depending on when they were last maintained, may need ridge tile re-pointing, new felt underlay or guttering replacement. There are also newer developments on the outskirts of the town where flat-roofed elements, modern tile profiles and EPDM systems are more common.

We cover Droitwich as part of our regular Worcestershire service area. It sits conveniently close to Bromsgrove, which we also cover, making the route from our Birmingham base practical for regular work. We carry out the full range of our services in Droitwich and can usually arrange a survey visit within a few days.
