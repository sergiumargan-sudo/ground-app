Roofing in Bromsgrove, Worcestershire

Bromsgrove is a thriving Worcestershire market town located just south of Birmingham, well connected by both road and rail. We cover Bromsgrove regularly as part of our service area — it’s a straightforward run down the A38 from our Northfield base, and we carry out work in the town and in the surrounding villages including Stoke Prior, Redditch Road and the wider Bromsgrove District on a regular basis.

Bromsgrove’s housing is genuinely diverse in terms of age and style. The historic town centre and older residential streets feature Victorian and Edwardian properties, many with clay tile roofs and original chimney stacks that need ongoing attention. The substantial post-war and late 20th century residential development that expanded Bromsgrove considerably brought a wide range of housing including concrete tile semi-detacheds, detached family homes and bungalows. More recent developments have added modern-style homes with a mix of flat and pitched roofing elements.

This variety means that virtually every type of roofing work we carry out elsewhere in our area comes up in Bromsgrove too. GRP flat roof extensions on modern homes, ridge tile re-pointing on post-war semis, lead flashing repairs on older Victorian properties and full re-roofs on homes approaching the end of their covering’s lifespan — we handle all of these regularly in the Bromsgrove area.
