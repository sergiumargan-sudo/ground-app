Roofing in Stirchley, Birmingham (B30)

Stirchley is a dense, characterful inner suburb in the south of Birmingham, occupying a long strip of the B30 postcode between Kings Heath to the east, Bournville to the west and Northfield further south. The area grew rapidly during the Edwardian era, driven by expansion along the Bristol Road and Pershore Road corridors, and its housing stock is dominated by the terraced and semi-detached red-brick houses typical of Birmingham’s early 20th century residential development. In recent years Stirchley has seen considerable regeneration and investment, bringing new residents to a neighbourhood that retains a strong community character.

The roofing on Stirchley’s Edwardian housing stock presents a well-understood set of maintenance challenges. The clay plain tile roofs on these properties are now well over a hundred years old, and while original tiles were made to an excellent standard, the supporting structure — battens, felt underlay and ridge mortar — has not always kept pace with the tile longevity. A significant proportion of Stirchley’s terrace roofs have either been partially or fully re-roofed in concrete interlocking tiles at some point in the last forty years, which can create an aesthetic mismatch but generally performs adequately in structural terms. Many properties still have original or partially-original clay tile roofs that benefit from careful like-for-like repairs rather than wholesale replacement.

Chimneys are a defining feature on Stirchley’s terraces. Most properties have either two shared stacks with the neighbouring terrace or, on end-of-terrace properties, a single prominent side stack. Lead flashing at the chimney junction is a common source of water ingress, and the mortar pointing between the brickwork courses on these older stacks deteriorates steadily with weathering. We carry out chimney repairs and lead flashing work throughout Stirchley.

The area also has a large number of flat-roofed rear extensions, ranging from original scullery extensions to more recent kitchen extensions and bathroom additions. GRP flat roof replacement is one of the most common jobs we carry out across Stirchley.
