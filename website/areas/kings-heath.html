Roofing in King’s Heath, Birmingham (B14)

King’s Heath is a well-established and popular south Birmingham suburb sitting in the B14 postcode, bounded broadly by Moseley to the north, Stirchley to the west and Solihull to the east. The area grew substantially during the Victorian and Edwardian periods and retains a rich stock of period housing — terraced streets, bay-fronted semis and a number of larger detached villas — that is broadly representative of Birmingham’s suburban expansion in the late 19th and early 20th century. The high street has long served as an independent retail and community hub, and the area continues to attract homeowners who value both the period character of the housing and its convenient location within south Birmingham.

The roofing challenges in King’s Heath are closely linked to the period character of the housing stock. Victorian and Edwardian terraces and semis typically have clay plain tile roofs with original or subsequent natural slate on the steeper-pitched sections. The chimneys on these properties are invariably present and often require maintenance: pointing between the brickwork courses, re-bedding of the flaunching around the chimney pots, and renewing lead flashings at the junction between the chimney stack and the roof slope are all regular requirements. Cast iron guttering on the older properties is another recurring source of calls — many homeowners choose to replace original cast iron with UPVC when a section fails, and we carry out this work throughout B14.

The interwar period also left a significant mark on King’s Heath, and there are large numbers of 1930s semi-detached houses with the characteristic steeply-pitched gable ends and bay window extensions that are so common across south Birmingham. These properties present typical concrete tile and flat roof extension maintenance requirements. More recent properties on the infill sites and newer streets are also part of our regular workload in the area.

We are based in south Birmingham and work regularly throughout the B14 postcode. Free surveys are available and we always carry out all work personally — no subcontracting.
