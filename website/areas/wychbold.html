Roofing in Wychbold, Worcestershire

Wychbold sits astride the A38 between Droitwich and Bromsgrove, close to Junction 5 of the M5 — which makes it easy to reach from both directions and puts it firmly within our regular Worcestershire coverage area. The village is perhaps best known locally for Webbs of Wychbold, one of the region’s largest garden centres, but it also has a varied and interesting mix of residential property that keeps us busy across all our services. Older village buildings sit alongside substantial 20th-century residential development, creating a cross-section of roofing needs that ranges from Victorian chimney stacks and original clay tiles through to concrete interlocking tiles on post-war semis.

A good proportion of Wychbold’s housing stock dates from the post-war decades, and many of these properties are now at the stage where flat-roofed extensions and garage roofs need replacing rather than patching. We regularly install Dryseal GRP flat roofing systems on exactly these kinds of extensions, providing a long-lasting, maintenance-free solution that is far superior to the felt systems originally specified. The concrete interlocking tile roofs on 1960s and 1970s houses in the area also commonly need ridge tile re-bedding and pointing, along with guttering and fascia replacement as the original systems come to the end of their life. We carry out all of these services and can survey any property in Wychbold free of charge.
