Roofing in Northfield, Birmingham (B31)

Northfield (B31) is one of our most active areas — we’ve been working on its streets and rooftops for over 15 years and know the local housing stock better than almost anywhere we cover. If you’re looking for a roofer in Northfield who actually knows the area, look no further.

Northfield (B31) is a south Birmingham suburb with a genuinely diverse range of housing. The area includes post-war semi-detached houses, council-built properties from the 1950s and 60s, 1970s and 80s estates, and a mix of newer developments. This variety means we encounter everything from ageing concrete tile roofs on bungalows to modern flat-roofed extensions, cast iron guttering on older stock and UPVC systems on newer builds.

The post-war housing in Northfield typically features concrete interlocking tiles on pitched roofs, with felt underlay that is often reaching the end of its serviceable life on properties that haven’t been re-roofed. Single-storey flat-roofed extensions are very common in Northfield and we carry out a steady stream of Dryseal GRP flat roof installations and replacements across the area.

We work in Northfield regularly and can usually offer a same-day or next-day response for surveys in the B31 area. For emergency call-outs in Northfield, we aim to respond within hours.
