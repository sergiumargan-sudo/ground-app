Roofing in Cutnall Green, Worcestershire

Cutnall Green is a quiet rural village in the Elmbridge and Rushock parish, set in the agricultural countryside between Droitwich and Kidderminster. This is classic Worcestershire farmland: gently rolling fields, hedgerow-lined lanes and a scattered community of traditional brick-and-tile properties. The village sits away from major roads and is the kind of place where finding a reliable local roofer willing to travel out can be a challenge — which is exactly why we make sure we cover it. We are based near Droitwich and Cutnall Green falls comfortably within our regular working area.

The properties in and around Cutnall Green are predominantly older rural buildings — farmhouses, farm cottages and converted outbuildings — constructed in the local tradition of red Worcestershire brick with clay plain tile roofs. These older clay tile roofs have original chimney stacks that are a characteristic feature of every farmhouse in the area, and the mortar pointing on both stacks and ridge tiles needs periodic attention, particularly on properties that have not been properly maintained for some years. We carry out full roof inspections, repoint ridges and stacks with the correct mortar mix, source matching clay plain tiles for repairs, and install new lead flashings where the existing lead has cracked or lifted.
