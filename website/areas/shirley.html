Roofing in Shirley, Solihull (B90)

Shirley is a large residential suburb in the south of the Solihull borough, situated in the B90 postcode area. It’s a popular area for families, with a strong mix of detached, semi-detached and terraced housing that spans several decades of development — from 1930s semis near the town centre through to 1970s and 1980s estates further out.

The 1960s to 1980s housing in Shirley is particularly active for us. Concrete interlocking tile roofs from this era are reaching the age where the mortar beds at the ridge are failing, the felt underlay is deteriorating, and re-roofing becomes necessary rather than optional. Single-storey flat-roofed extensions are also very common on Shirley properties of this period, and GRP fibreglass replacement is the most frequent flat roofing job we carry out here.

Properties closer to the Solihull border in the Shirley area include more substantial detached houses with clay tile roofs and larger chimneys, where regular maintenance including lead flashing inspection and chimney pointing is important. We are experienced with all these property types and carry out everything from small repairs to full re-roofing projects in Shirley.
