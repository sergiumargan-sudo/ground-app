Roofing in Quinton, Birmingham (B32)

Quinton is a south-west Birmingham suburb sitting in the B32 postcode area, bordered by Harborne to the east and Halesowen to the west. It’s a predominantly residential area characterised by a pleasing mix of 1930s semi-detached and detached properties, with some larger Edwardian houses on the higher ground and a number of more modern estates and bungalows towards the outer edges of the suburb.

The 1930s housing that makes up much of Quinton presents a particular set of roofing considerations. Many properties of this era still have their original concrete interlocking tiles, and while the tiles themselves are often still sound, the sand and cement mortar beds on ridge and hip tiles tend to deteriorate significantly after 30–40 years. We see a lot of ridge tile repairs and re-beds in Quinton, particularly on properties that haven’t had roofing work done since they were built. Original felt underlay on these properties is typically also at or near the end of its life.

We’re regularly working in Quinton and know the area’s housing stock well. Whether it’s a GRP flat roof on a rear extension, re-pointing a chimney on a 1930s semi or replacing deteriorating UPVC guttering along the eaves, we can usually survey and quote quickly and get work scheduled without a long wait.
