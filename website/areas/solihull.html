Roofing in Solihull, West Midlands

Solihull is one of the most prosperous areas of the West Midlands, situated to the east of Birmingham. The borough is known for its large detached and semi-detached properties, many of which were built during the major residential expansions of the 1930s through to the 1980s. These homes typically feature pitched concrete or clay tile roofs, with flat-roofed garages and extensions attached — exactly the kind of work we do every day.

Older Solihull properties — particularly in and around the town centre, Knowle and Dorridge — tend to have clay plain tiles or natural slate on steeply pitched roofs, with original chimneys requiring regular maintenance. The lead flashings, chimney pointing and ridge work on these period properties needs careful, experienced hands.

Across the broader borough, the more modern estates in areas like Chelmsley Wood and Kingshurst include a significant volume of flat-roofed housing and extensions where Dryseal GRP is the right modern replacement choice.

We cover the whole Solihull borough — including the town centre, Shirley, Knowle, Dorridge, Balsall Common, Chelmsley Wood and all surrounding areas. Free surveys available across all Solihull postcodes.
